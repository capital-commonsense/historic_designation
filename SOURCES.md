# Sources

Data collections used in this analysis. Links are the addresses the pipeline fetched from (`src/historic_preservation/config.py` and `records_request/manual_fetch.md`).

## DC designations, property and permits

- [DC GIS map services](https://maps2.dcgis.dc.gov/dcgis/rest/services) (DC Office of the Chief Technology Officer): historic district and landmark layers, zoning lots, the real property tax roll (ITSPE: assessments and tax billing history), condominium crosswalks, address points and Department of Buildings building permits
- [DC Inventory of Historic Sites and its updates](https://planning.dc.gov/publication/dc-inventory-updates) (DC Office of Planning)
- [Pending nominations to the DC Inventory](https://planning.dc.gov/page/pending-nominations-dc-inventory) (DC Historic Preservation Office)
- [DC Historic Preservation Office annual reports, FY1979–2023](https://planning.dc.gov/publications) (DC Office of Planning; each year at `planning.dc.gov/publication/<year>-hpo-annual-report`)

## Review board and Mayor's Agent decisions

- [Historic Preservation Review Board meeting records](https://planning.dc.gov/publications) (DC Office of Planning): actions, agendas, notices and staff reports (search "HPRB")
- HPRB meeting archive, 2006–2026 (DC Historic Preservation Office, box.com) <!-- TODO: add the share link -->
- [Mayor's Agent for Historic Preservation decisions](https://law.digital.georgetown.edu/collections/932f9f5b-a884-4c9c-be30-95211f86b424) (Georgetown Law Library)

## Courts and zoning

- [CourtListener](https://www.courtlistener.com) (Free Law Project): DC Court of Appeals opinions and federal court dockets (RECAP)
- [DC Office of Zoning case records](https://app.dcoz.dc.gov/CaseReport/ViewExhibitsReport.aspx): Zoning Commission case exhibits

## DC Preservation League

- [DC Preservation League Form 990s](https://projects.propublica.org/nonprofits/organizations/521038849) (ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer, EIN 52-1038849)
- [DC Preservation League](https://dcpreservation.org): nominations, endangered places, grant award lists, board and history pages

## Comparison cities

- [Census 2020 place boundaries and water areas](https://tigerweb.geo.census.gov/arcgis/rest/services/TIGERweb) (U.S. Census Bureau, TIGERweb)
- New York Landmarks Preservation Commission on [NYC Open Data](https://data.cityofnewyork.us): [individual landmarks](https://data.cityofnewyork.us/d/buis-pvji), [historic districts](https://data.cityofnewyork.us/d/skyk-mpzq); [address points](https://data.cityofnewyork.us/d/uf93-f8nk)
- Philadelphia Historical Commission register sites and historic districts ([City of Philadelphia ArcGIS Online](https://services.arcgis.com/fLeGjb7u4uXqeF9q/arcgis/rest/services)); property points from the [city's Carto server](https://phl.carto.com/api/v2/sql) (Office of Property Assessment)
- [Boston landmarks, historic districts and SAM addresses](https://gisportal.boston.gov/arcgis/rest/services) (City of Boston GIS)
- [OpenStreetMap building extracts](https://download.geofabrik.de/north-america/us/) (Geofabrik)
